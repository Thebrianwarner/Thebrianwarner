THE STAMP

THEBRIANWARNER™ is a standard.

All caps. No spaces. Trademark attached. It started as a description of a certain kind of athlete, and it became a standard for how to operate.

Football locker rooms, film rooms, huddles, weight rooms, federal law enforcement, defense investigations, courtrooms, interview rooms, leadership meetings, family conversations and personal adversity. Each room added its own vocabulary, decision rules and performance standards. Phrases like “step up into the pocket,” “ball security” and “move the chains” aren't decoration. They're field-tested operating language.

The mission: teach regular Joe and regular Jane to develop an operating system for pressure, taught by proximity through championship conversation.

WHAT IT IS

Lead with the pressure.

You don't have to explain your job title. Bring the pressure you're under right now. We work from there.

It isn't:

  • Therapy
  • Consulting
  • Motivational speaking
  • Traditional life coaching

HOW IT RUNS

Four downs.

  1. 1ST

    Accept the invitation

    Pick a time. This is an offer, not a sales pitch.

  2. 2ND

    Get your field position

    Who you are right now, where you're playing from, and what scoreboard you've been using.

  3. 3RD

    Review the film

    The pressure on you and the penalties that keep showing up. Named plainly.

  4. 4TH

    Call the next play

    You leave with your next first down. Roger that.
